**Skills:** SolidWorks, Iterative Design, Technical Report Writing, 3D Printing

**Objective:** Create a mechanically operated toy for the ME100 course final project.

**Outcome:** Worked on a team to design and prototype Digit, a wearable mechanical thumb capable of actuation and grasping. The purpose of the project was to create a toy using the engineering design process while applying 3D modelling and machine shop skills learned in class. We also assessed and wrote a report on the toy’s mechanical viability for potential further development by a company.

**Experience:** I was responsible for several core mechanical components including designing the mounting mechanism of the toy to the user’s hand and an elastic extension mechanism that restores the thumb to its neutral position. I modelled the mount in SolidWorks and created iterations of the components based on functional testing. In my initial design, I proposed the idea of having a component that slid onto the user’s thumb and attached to a string that was used to move Digit. During testing, I noticed that this configuration defeated Digit’s role as an additional finger since it relied on the user’s thumb for actuation. To address this, I transitioned to wrist based actuation to free all fingers for use while enabling controlled movement of Digit which we used for the final iteration.

**Learning:** I learned how to create independent mechanical motion paths that could be connected to specific hand movements, preventing wrist rotation from unintentionally triggering extension or retraction of Digit, and learned to use tension and varying string lengths to actuate Digit.
