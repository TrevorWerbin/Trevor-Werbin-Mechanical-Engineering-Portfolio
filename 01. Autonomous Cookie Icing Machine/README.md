**Skills:** SolidWorks, C++, Fabrication, DFM, Circuits, Machining Safety, 3D Printing

**Objective:** Design, build, and program an autonomous cookie icing machine capable of applying a user designed multi-colour icing pattern to the surface of a cookie in <4 minutes.

**Outcome:** Worked on a four person team to design and build a dual-axis autonomous cookie icing machine consisting of VEX IQ electronics, a gantry system and tri-colour rotating syringe barrel, driven by a C++ program. We also created a custom HTML interface for users to draw custom designs to send to the machine.
 
**Experience:** I contributed to the 3D modelling of the gantry and icing dispenser in SolidWorks and was responsible for majority of the fabrication, including 3D printing components, such as the motor casings and gantry system, and laser cutting the acrylic racks, baseplate, and enclosure panels. Our initial idea was to have a design where the nozzle moves and the baseplate remains still in the x-y position. Due to having a limited amount of motors, I proposed the idea of moving the baseplate that holds the cookie instead of the icing dispenser to improve icing accuracy and reduce the load on the upper motors. By conducting iterative testing, I also discovered and implemented the logic on the motor-driven plunger to avoid failed dispensing caused by trapped air in the syringe and icing pooling after drawing a figure. Throughout, I applied DFM principles to optimize parts to be low-cost and manufactured quickly while remaining strong.

**Learning:** I learned the importance of proper calibration of sensors for reliable autonomous operation since they’re very sensitive to their environment and often don’t behave exactly as stated on the data sheet.
