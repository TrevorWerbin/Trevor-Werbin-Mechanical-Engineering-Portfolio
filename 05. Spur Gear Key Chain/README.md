**Skills:** SolidWorks, Machine Shop Equipment Operation

**Objective:** Design and fabricate a custom keychain.

**Outcome:** Fabricated a 3.5 x 1.25 x 0.5” keychain with a revolving spur gear.

**Experience:** I modelled the full assembly in SolidWorks and utilized a lathe, 3-axis mill machine, drill press and a tap (for internal threads) to create and assemble the part.

**Learning:** I learned to execute clean and precise machining and learned about proper machine shop etiquette and safety. I also learned to model physical mechanisms in an assembly in SolidWorks.
